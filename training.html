Safe Browsing Hygiene

Core Protocol

Techniques to avoid malicious redirects, weaponized links, and deceptive top-level domains.

  • Inspect full domain endings before entering credentials (watch for `.xyz`, `.top`).
  • Never log in via raw IP address links (e.g., `http://192.168.x.x`).
  • Verify SSL certificates on banking and campus portals.
🔍

Phishing Red Flags

Email Defense

Identify social engineering signals and urgency coercion used by cyber adversaries.

  • Look for artificial urgency like "account suspended within 24 hours".
  • Watch for unverified sender aliases spoofing internal authorities.
  • Refuse macro activation requests on email attachments (`.xlsm`, `.bat`).
🔐

Identity & Credentials

Access Security

Prevent credential leakage across educational institutions and workplace environments.

  • Enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) via hardware keys or authenticator apps.
  • Never share one-time OTP passwords with phone or email callers.
  • Isolate enterprise/student credentials from third-party non-academic sites.
